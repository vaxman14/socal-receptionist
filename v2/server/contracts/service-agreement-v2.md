This Service Agreement (the "Agreement") is between **SOCAL RECEPTIONIST LLC**, a California limited liability company ("Provider," "we," "us"), and the business identified in the onboarding account and signature block ("Client," "you").

By electronically signing, you agree to the following terms.

## 1. Service

Provider supplies a self-service AI telephone receptionist. Depending on Client settings, the service may answer inbound calls, provide Client-supplied business information, capture caller details, email call and lead summaries, transfer calls to a Client-supplied number, and schedule appointments through an optional Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook/Microsoft 365 calendar connection.

The service does not include custom software integrations, practice-management integrations, CRM integrations, or done-for-you onboarding unless separately agreed in writing.

## 2. Self-Service Setup and Support

Client uses Provider's conversational AI onboarding assistant to supply and confirm business information and activate the account. Client is responsible for reviewing the extracted information before activation and keeping it accurate. Support is available at support@socalreceptionist.com when assistance is needed.

## 3. Free Trial

Each eligible new Client account receives one thirty (30) day free trial beginning when the receptionist is activated. No payment card is required to start the trial. If Client does not add a valid payment method and select a paid billing option before the trial expires, the service may be suspended automatically.

If Client adds a payment method during the trial, no subscription charge is due before the displayed trial end date. Client may cancel before the trial ends to avoid the first charge. Provider may deny repeat, duplicate, fraudulent, or abusive trials.

## 4. Fees and Automatic Renewal

After the free trial, Client may choose either:

- **Monthly:** $69 per month; or
- **Annual:** $690 per year.

There is no setup fee. Recurring fees are billed in advance through Stripe and renew automatically for the selected billing period until canceled. Applicable taxes may be added. Client authorizes Provider and Stripe to charge the payment method on file when the trial ends and on each renewal date.

Provider may change pricing with at least thirty (30) days' notice. A price change will apply no earlier than the next renewal after the notice period.

## 5. Cancellation and Refunds

Client may cancel through the Stripe billing portal at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period unless applicable law requires otherwise. Fees already charged are non-refundable except where required by law. Service remains available through the end of the entitled trial or paid period unless suspended for abuse, unlawful use, or security reasons.

## 6. Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to:

- Provide accurate and current hours, services, policies, pricing, transfer numbers, and notification addresses;
- Review how the AI represents the Client's business;

- Obtain any notices, consents, or permissions required for call recording, messaging, appointment invitations, and customer communications;

Client should not treat a Provider-provisioned number as portable or permanent unless confirmed in writing.

The service depends on third-party networks and processors and may occasionally be delayed or unavailable. Provider does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free service.

The AI is not a licensed professional and does not provide legal, medical, financial, tax, or other professional advice. Client is responsible for the business information supplied to the AI and for reviewing the service's configuration.

## 9. Calendar Connections and Data

Calendar connection is optional. If Client connects Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook/Microsoft 365, Client authorizes Provider to access availability and create appointment events only as needed to operate the scheduling feature. If no calendar is connected or scheduling is disabled, the receptionist will capture caller details for Client follow-up instead.

Provider processes account, caller, transcript, recording, and appointment information as described in the Privacy Policy and may use service providers including Twilio, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Supabase, Resend, and Stripe to operate the service.
